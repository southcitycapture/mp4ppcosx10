port/src/thp/thp_jpeg.c -- attribution
======================================

The THP movie decoder (port/src/thp/thp_jpeg.c, M38) is written for this
port, but three of its parts follow stb_image.h by Sean Barrett
(https://github.com/nothings/stb), and are credited to it:

  * the integer inverse DCT (the IDCT_1D butterfly and its 12-bit fixed-point
    constants; stb_image derives it from the IJG's jidctint.c "islow");
  * the Huffman decoder's 9-bit lookahead table and the AC "fast_ac" table
    that folds a short code's run, size and value into one entry;
  * the sign-extension trick of `extend_receive` (the jbias / bmask tables).

The THP-specific parts -- no byte stuffing, THPDec.c's restart handling, the
GX I8 tiled output, the colour conversion with THPDraw.c's TEV constants and
the triangle-filtered chroma -- are this port's.
